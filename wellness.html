Good To Know

Common Questions

Is this a substitute for talking to my doctor?

No. The quiz gives a personalized starting point based on your answers. These are supplement suggestions, not medical advice — we always recommend keeping your prescribing physician in the loop.

How does billing work?

Your plan is billed automatically each month to the card on file and shipped to your door. The $29.99 enrollment fee is currently waived as a launch promotion.

Can I cancel or change my plan?

Yes. You can cancel with 30 days' notice, and your formula can be adjusted anytime as your needs change over time.

What's the difference between Essential and Premium?

Essential is a high-quality hybrid formula that's cost-conscious, using premium ingredients where they matter most. Premium uses 100% Xymogen, practitioner-grade ingredients throughout.
