Good To Know

Common Questions

Is this a substitute for talking to my doctor?

No. The quiz gives a personalized starting point based on your answers. These are supplement suggestions, not medical advice — we always recommend keeping your prescribing physician in the loop.

Do I have to subscribe?

No. There's no subscription. You order your supplements on WholeScripts a month at a time and pay there — nothing auto-charges, and you're never locked in.

How do I order after the quiz?

Three steps: 1) create a free account under our pharmacy on WholeScripts, 2) search each product from your plan (we show you the exact name and a Copy button) and add it to your cart, 3) check out. Your results page walks you through it — or call us and a pharmacist will set it up with you.

What will it cost?

You pay WholeScripts directly for the supplements you order, and prices show once you're signed in. There's no membership fee and no subscription — you only pay for what you add to your cart.

Why WholeScripts and Xymogen?

Xymogen is a practitioner-grade supplement line sold through the WholeScripts dispensary, not in retail stores. Registering under our pharmacy links your WholeScripts account to our pharmacists, who set your plan and check it against your therapy.
