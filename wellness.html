Good To Know

Common Questions

Is this a substitute for talking to my doctor?

No. The quiz gives a personalized starting point based on your answers. These are supplement suggestions, not medical advice — we always recommend keeping your prescribing physician in the loop.

Do I have to subscribe?

No. There's no subscription. You order your supplements on WholeScripts a month at a time and pay there — nothing auto-charges, and you're never locked in.

How do I order after the quiz?

The quiz matches you to one of our protocols — a set of supplements our pharmacist has already put together on WholeScripts — and your results page gives you the two links you need. 1) Create your free WholeScripts account using the first link. It opens a page that already lists My Best Pharmacy as your practitioner, so there's no referral code to type in — and it's what connects your account to us, so do this one first. 2) Open your protocol with the second link. Everything in it is already bundled, so you press Add to Cart once instead of searching for products one at a time. 3) Choose monthly delivery if you want it to arrive automatically, then check out and pay WholeScripts directly. Rather not do it alone? Call us and a pharmacist will walk you through it.

What will it cost?

You pay WholeScripts directly for the supplements you order, and prices show once you're signed in. There's no membership fee and no subscription — you only pay for what you add to your cart.

Why WholeScripts and Xymogen?

Xymogen is a practitioner-grade supplement line sold through the WholeScripts dispensary, not in retail stores. Registering under our pharmacy links your WholeScripts account to My Best Pharmacy.
