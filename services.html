Understanding Your Medication

What Is a Compounded Medication?

A compounded medication is one that's made specifically for you by our pharmacists — not mass-manufactured. Your doctor prescribed it because the commercially available options don't meet your exact needs.

This could mean a different dose, a different delivery form (like a cream instead of a pill), a combination of ingredients, or a drug that isn't available commercially.

My Best Pharmacy is a licensed compounding pharmacy in the State of Florida, and we prepare each compound to your prescriber's exact specification.

Custom Strength & Dosage

Cream, capsule, gel, troche, suppository — whatever works for you.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring a paper prescription?

Yes. Please bring a paper prescription copy with you when you come to pick up your medication. Your doctor can also fax a copy to 561.200.4236 in advance, but we still require a physical prescription on file at the pharmacy.

How long does it take to fill a compounded prescription?

Compounded medications are made fresh to order and typically take longer than standard prescriptions. Timeframes vary by formulation — we'll give you an estimate when your prescription comes in and contact you when it's ready.

Does my insurance cover compounded medications?

Compounded medications are cash-pay only — we do not bill insurance for custom compounds. For standard retail prescriptions, we work with select benefit programs including Nations Benefits. Please call us with questions about your specific situation.

Can you deliver to my home?

Yes. We offer delivery service throughout South Florida and ship statewide. Ask us about delivery options when you call or when you pick up your first prescription.

What if I have questions about my medication?

Call us at (561) 200-4245 during business hours. Our pharmacists are available to answer questions about your compound — how to store it, how to use it, what to expect.

Is my information private?

Yes. We follow all HIPAA requirements and maintain the same confidentiality standards as any licensed pharmacy. Your medical information is never shared without your authorization.

Accepted Benefit Programs

We accept Nations Benefits cards — eligible Medicare Advantage members can use their benefit allowance for OTC health products, vitamins, supplements, and medical equipment at our pharmacy.

Express
Scripts®

We are an Express Scripts participating pharmacy. If your insurance plan uses Express Scripts as its pharmacy benefit manager, you can fill your retail prescriptions with us.
